Help & About

PittCo Fishing is a nationwide fishing operating system for planning, logging, mapping and sharing without giving away private fishing spots.

Core fishing tools

Catch logging with photos and GPS, trip journals, tackle setups, tournaments, map overlays, private waypoints and live weather remain the foundation of the app.

Open Fishing ToolsExplore Lakes

Nationwide lake intelligence

Search U.S. bass fisheries, filter by state or region, save favorites and recent lakes, find nearby water and open lake-specific weather, maps and Community boards.

Privacy by default

Exact catch GPS and private waypoints are not public by default. Community posts default to lake-level context; exact sharing must be intentional.

Community & moderation

Lake reports, catch posts, tournament talk, tackle discussion, saves, reactions and local reporting are available. Account-backed follows and server-side moderation remain a backend phase.
