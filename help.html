PITTCO FISHING

Help & About

PittCo Fishing is being built as a nationwide fishing operating system for planning, logging, mapping and sharing without giving away private fishing spots.

Core fishing tools

Catch logging with photos and GPS, trip journals, tackle setups, tournaments, map overlays, private waypoints and live weather remain the foundation of the app.

Open Fishing ToolsExplore Lakes

Nationwide lake intelligence

Search major U.S. bass fisheries, filter by region, save favorites and recent lakes, find nearby water and open lake-specific weather, maps and Community boards. The data model is designed to grow to thousands of lakes.

Privacy by default

Exact catch GPS and private waypoints are not public by default. Community posts default to lake-level location context, and exact sharing must be intentional.

Community & moderation

Lake-specific reports, catch posts, tournament talk, tackle discussion, saves, reactions and reporting are scaffolded. Account-backed follows and server-side moderation are planned for the backend phase.
